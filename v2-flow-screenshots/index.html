Asas · design and flows

Every screen of Asas on phone and laptop, for design review

Asas is a construction company's daily reporting app: engineers fill site reports from their phones, administrators build the forms, assign them, send reminders and follow progress on dashboards and reports. Arabic first, English too, light and dark.

26 screens · 90 screenshots Phone 390 px · Laptop 1440 px Arabic (right to left) and English Captured 14 Nov 2026

About this file

  • Production is what the client uses today. Staging only is built and tested but not released yet (stage progress). Changed on staging marks production screens that look different on staging.
  • Screenshots come from the staging version running on a copy of staging's data, so they show everything that exists. Project 119 is real-shaped sample data; "Hazim" is the field engineer, "Admin" the administrator.
  • Each screen card lists its address, who sees it, what it does, other states it has, and notes for the designer. Click any screenshot to open it full size.
  • Breakpoint: below 1024 px the app uses the phone shell (bottom tab bar); from 1024 px the laptop shell (side menu). Every tappable target is at least 44 × 44 px on a phone.

Phone shell

Bottom tab bar: Today (with a due-count badge), Forms, My entries, Settings. Forms open full screen with a step header and a fixed bottom action. Pickers open as bottom sheets.

Laptop shell

264 px side menu: search (Ctrl/Cmd+K), Today, form groups, Administration (administrators), profile at the bottom. Content area up to about 1100 px; details open as side drawers.

Standalone pages

Executive Overview opens in its own tab as a 16:9 canvas. Sign in, session ended and 404 are full-page states.

Main flows

1 · A site engineer's day

flowchart LR
  A[WhatsApp / Telegram reminder at 9:00] --> B[Open Asas: Today]
  B --> C[Fill: daily progress report]
  C --> D[Step 1 Basics] --> E[Step 2 Progress: stage blocks] --> G[Step 3 Photos + review]
  G -->|online| H[Sent: done for today]
  G -->|no connection| I[Saved on this phone] -->|connection back| H
  C -.->|leave mid-way| J[Draft kept on the phone]
  H --> K[My entries]

2 · Setting up work (administrator)

flowchart LR
  A[Form builder: create or edit form] --> B[Steps, fields, rules, access] --> C[Publish]
  C --> D[Form assignments: pick people, dates]
  D --> E[New-assignment message sent]
  F[Messages: reminder times, days, channels] --> G[Reminders to those who haven't filled]
  D --> H[Who filled and when: weekly grid]
  H --> I[Remind those who haven't filled now]

3 · Following progress and reporting

flowchart LR
  A[Daily reports arrive] --> B[Executive Overview: tiles, charts, filters]
  B --> C[Project progress: stage by stage]
  B --> D[Copy share link: office screen / owner]
  A --> E[Stakeholder report: daily, weekly, monthly PDF + written analysis]
  E --> F[WhatsApp to recipients]
  G[Messages: report times and recipients] --> E
  H[Stakeholder reports page: preview / send now / history] --> E

4 · Stage progress (staging)

flowchart LR
  A[Projects form: floors, parking, sides] --> B[Project's stage plan copied]
  B --> C[Daily report: choose stage, level or side; plan met; finished]
  C --> D[Days earned per stage]
  D --> E[Project progress page] --> F[Reopen a finished unit]
  D --> G[Overview: work complete, behind/ahead, forecast]
  H[Pause log: pause / resume project] --> D

Filling a form (daily progress report)

The daily progress report is the core of the app. On a phone it is three steps with a fixed bottom button; on a laptop the same sections sit on one long page with a single Send. On staging it has stage blocks (stage progress); production still has the earlier version of this form. Drafts save on the phone automatically; entries sent without a connection wait on the phone and send themselves later.

Issues spotted while capturing

Found on the staging version (stage progress, not yet released). Not fixed yet.

  1. Entry detail on a phone repeats the stage block: "Stage 1" appears inside "Basics" as well as under "Progress".
  2. Entry detail on a phone is wider than the screen: a long question ("Did this stage finish today on the selected floors or sides?") is cut off and the page can slide sideways.
  3. Executive Overview on a phone shows the 16:9 canvas very small with large empty space above and below (expected today; see question 2).

Questions for the design review

  1. Step 2 of the daily report (stage blocks) is the longest screen: is the hierarchy clear enough for one-hand use on site?
  2. Executive Overview on a phone shrinks the whole 16:9 canvas. Should it get a phone layout (stacked tiles and charts)?
  3. Admin pages on a phone (form builder, users, messages) work but are dense. Which deserve a phone-first layout, and which can stay laptop-first?
  4. Arabic typography: Tajawal for everything, including numbers and dates. Is the scale and weight right for long Arabic labels?
  5. Sign-in and first impression: brand presence, and preparing for self sign-up in v3.
  6. Empty, loading and error states share one style (see the component sheet). Are they consistent and friendly enough?
  7. Charts in dark mode and colour meaning (accent vs success vs danger) across tiles, grids and bars.
  8. Icons: a small hand-drawn set in the app; is a fuller, consistent icon set needed?
  9. Messages outside the app (WhatsApp templates, Telegram, the PDF report): should they be part of this review?
